# Word lists

`en.txt` and `fa.txt` (word, Zipf frequency) are generated at build time with `tools/make_wordlists.py` from
[wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) by Robyn Speer, whose data is licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Those generated files are therefore
under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well; the rest of the project is MIT.
